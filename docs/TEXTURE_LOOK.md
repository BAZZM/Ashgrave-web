# Texture look test (lane G2)

In normal play only the ground, water and trees load textures (Full quality only; see "Decision and what shipped"). The look test is behind a debug argument; `game/scripts/tex_kit.gd` holds the shared materials, `tex_looktest.gd` the scene.

**Run:** `python3 tools/build.py --looktest` (the release pack leaves out the look test's extra textures), then `node tools/tex_shots.js [--treatments=flat,grain,source] [--cams=v1,w2] [--single] [--scale=2]`. Pictures land in `/home/claude/screens/g2/<treatment>/NN-name.png` (844x390, device scale 2), the memory lines in `mem.txt`, and the flat | grain | source sheets in `compare/`. One client: `node tools/check.js --probe "--looktest=tex --treatment=grain --ltcam=v6b-duel-armour --ltmem"`. Textures: `python3 tools/pack_textures.py`, then `python3 tools/import_assets.py`.

## The treatments
- **FLAT**: today's flat and vertex colour; the control, and the fallback whenever `Run.quality_allows("textures")` is false (nothing is loaded then).
- **GRAIN**: luminance-only grain, `mix(dark, light, g)`, light = tint x (1+d), dark = tint x (1-d) mixed 10% to fog, d+0.10 if tint V < 0.25; grain stretched 5th-95th percentile to 0.10-0.90; macro noise +-8%, 37 m. Six channel-packed textures. d: grass 0.18, forest 0.20, mud 0.20, trail 0.28, water 0.15, stone 0.28, cobble 0.22, wood and bark 0.25, leaf 0.25, armour 0.30 hero / 0.20 enemy.
- **SOURCE**: the colour files, graded: desaturate, hue against the texture's own mean, brightness gain softened to 0.55 of the texture's contrast, blended by k toward the palette tint. Desaturation / k: grass, forest 0.30 / 0.75 (0.25 forest), trail and mud 0.25 / 0.80, water 0.30 / 0.80, stone 0.20, bark 0.25, leaf 0.30, steel and chain 0.10, gambeson 0.25 (all k 0.80), cloth 0.10 / 0.25.

Tile, world units per repeat: ground 4 (second rotated sample 9.2 in GRAIN; SOURCE uses a sine warp instead), water 4 and 6.5, stone and cobble 4, planks 2, bark 2, leaf cards 1.6, armour 0.5, banner once (cropped, not tiled).

## Substituted
Texture arrays not used (two packs read twice instead). `source_color` dropped: Compatibility hands texels over undecoded, so means are raw. No tangents: water normals are perturbed in view space by hand. Instance uniforms avoided (a material per colour). Ashlands: forest-floor and mud grain under the Ashlands tint; reads as dark gritty soil, without cracks.

## Weaknesses
- GRAIN: soft and uniform at distance; walls and trees lose moss and colour; ripple normals are regular stripes; banner shows the grain only.
- SOURCE: busier, high-frequency grass; single sample per layer so repeats may show; trail reads pink; moss and clover stay; only four ground layers (meadow unused); extra 7.7 MB.
- Both: fighter / ground luminance (linear) flat 1.57, GRAIN 1.53 (armour overlay 1.47), SOURCE 1.58 (1.51); target 1.5. Texel density agrees between ground and walls (4 m tile). Banner crop and Greywood camera are unpolished. iPhone unverified.

## Trade-off
GRAIN costs 5 MB, matches the palette and the Art Bible, and gives subtle surface; it throws away the owner's colour. SOURCE costs 12.8 MB (about 2.5 MB compressed), keeps recognisable textures and gives the strongest surface detail; it looks busier and sits closer to the photographic risk.

## Decision and what shipped (lane G3)
The Visual Critic compared the three treatments and chose **GRAIN for ground and water**. Later lane decisions: trees GRAIN; walls and wood are decided after an iPhone test of compressed textures; the banner texture and the armour overlay are dropped (fighters stay flat). So the real game today textures ground, water and (lane G4a, below) trees; everything else stays flat.

**Quality gate.** `overworld_terrain.gd` builds ground and water textured only when `Run.quality_allows("textures")` is true (Full) and the textures are in the pack; otherwise it builds today's flat ground (vertex colour) and flat water plane and loads no texture (`TexKit.tex` asks first). The gate is live: when `Run.quality_rev` changes, the loaded chunks are re-meshed one per frame, and after the last swap to flat `TexKit.clear()` frees the textures. (Full after Safe still waits for the next launch, as the QUALITY button says.) `--flatground` keeps the flat look at Full, for before pictures.

**Pack.** Release pack ships only `packed/terrain_a`, `packed/terrain_b`, `packed/ripple` (lossless, 2.63 MB): `tools/build.py` leaves the other imported textures out unless `--looktest`; the look test then falls back to flat. Pack 732,719 B before, 3,509,934 B after (+2.78 MB, code included).

**Critic fixes applied.** (1) Water: two ripple samples at -20 and +7 degrees (the ripple map's own lines run at 45), normals turned back with them, ripples fade 6 to 15 m, water grain fades to its mean 25 to 60 m. (2) Shallow tint #3E6A68 only within about 1 m of the shore (baked from water depth, 1.25 m cells, tightened in the shader); elsewhere Deep #20343A, and beyond about 10 m the water is darkened further and loses its sky reflection so it stays darker than its bank. (3) Trail: its own warm dry-earth colour #6E5238 with grain depth 0.28 in the middle falling to 0.18 at its edges, drawn wider (4.2 m) so it holds to the tower. The Critic's #6B5A40 mixed into the ground made the trail vanish, which is a navigation regression, so the trail reads by hue (a clear shift from the grass) at about the grass's luminance. (4) Ashlands: trail #746A60 (pale warm grey-brown, about 1.35 times the ground); ground tint #605C5B, grain depth 0.12, highlights capped at 0.62 (no frost lines). (5) Greywood ground tint #566253 (was #445A40): rendered saturation 0.38 to about 0.28, hue unchanged. The hex #2F3F2C has the same hue and saturation as the old tint, so "toward it" could only mean less saturated under the region's green ambient. (6) Fighter / ground luminance (linear), measured in the real duel by `tools/g3_shots.js` + `tools/g3_measure.py` (fighter body, no blade or gold trim, against the ground under and around its feet): grass (Hearthvale) 1.64, trail 1.5 to 1.7 (it varies with the pose), mud 2.57, Greywood 2.72, Ashlands 3.15. Flat today by the same method: grass about 1.1, trail about 0.9. Hearthvale's ground tint is 5% darker (#596635) and the Meadows' 18% (#4A5831) when textured; fighters untouched. Whole-picture ground luminance against flat: Hearthvale 0.83, Greywood 1.05, Ashlands 1.08.

**Memory** (real game, 844x390, Full): texture memory +3.15 MB on land (4.32 to 7.47) and in a duel (6.27 to 9.41), video +3.6 MB; Safe adds 0.


## Trees in the real game (lane G4a)
**What shipped.** At Full quality every scattered tree (meadow, Greywood oaks and pines, marsh and ashlands dead trees, ruins) and every gatherable oak and pine takes the GRAIN bark and canopy treatment; at Safe they are today's flat trees and nothing is loaded.
- **Maps.** One new lossless pack, `packed/trees` (256 px, four channels: pine bark, deciduous bark, broadleaf canopy, evergreen canopy; `tools/pack_textures.py`), 190,496 B in the release pack and 349,524 B (0.33 MB) of texture memory. A canopy grain is the leaf spray's luminance where the spray is and a dark gap (0.25) elsewhere, so a solid canopy shows leaf clumps with shadow between them. The two bark grains were 256 px already, so nothing is upscaled.
- **Materials.** `TexKit.tree_mat(kind)`: one shader, six shared materials (`bark_dec`, `bark_pine`, `leaf_broad`, `leaf_pine`, `node_oak`, `node_pine`), created once and cached. The tint is the instance colour (MultiMesh) or the baked vertex colour (gatherable trees, one mesh told apart into trunk and crown by colour), so no material is made per tree or per chunk, and the palette colours are unchanged: a textured tree has the mean colour of the flat one (checked by eye on the before and after pictures). Object-space triplanar like `solid()`, grain depth 0.25 for bark and canopy, bark tile 2 m, canopy tile 0.9 m (the look test's 1.6 m read as oversized leaves in the Greywood shot). Pine trunks use the pine bark and oak and dead trunks the deciduous one, which makes the pine trunks a second MultiMesh batch per chunk, only at Full.
- **Quality gate.** The same rule and clear paths as the ground (`OverworldTerrain._trees_textured()`: `Run.quality_allows("textures")` and the pack has the map). Each chunk keeps its tree nodes; on a quality change `_apply_trees` puts the flat materials back chunk by chunk with the ground re-mesh, and `TexKit.clear()` then frees everything; a switch to Safe in the tower clears it in `world_hud.gd` `_toggle_quality()` as before. `check.js quality` now also wants at least 3 textures held at Full on land (ground and trees) and still 0 held at Safe. Debug: `--flattrees` (flat trees at Full, the before pictures), `--qswitch=SECONDS` (press the QUALITY handler and print what `TexKit` holds).
- **Foliage cards: built, shipped OFF.** Broadleaf only (never conifers, dead or burnt trees): one 6-card mesh per oak, one extra batch per chunk, `--cards` turns them on. Measured in Greywood (the `g3-greywood` view, 844x390, Full): draw calls 304 without cards, 309 with (+1.6%); primitives 90,496 and 91,108 (+0.7%); texture memory +0.33 MB more (`packed/foliage`). Both are far inside the limits (15% and 30%), but the cards look worse than no cards: sprays placed on the canopy surface hide inside it, and moved outward they read as detached tufts floating off the tree (see /home/claude/screens/g4a, round 1 and 2 pictures, not kept). So the release pack does not carry `packed/foliage` and the canopy shows grain only. A card made for the canopy edge (a wider leaf mass, not a single spray) would be the next try.
- **Budget.** Texture memory +0.33 MB in Full on land (limit 2.5 MB); download +0.19 MB (limit about 2 MB); with cards off. Running total of all textures (ground, water, trees): 3.48 MB of texture memory on land (3.15 + 0.33) and 2.82 MB of the pack, against the 16 MB budget.
- **Fighter against trees.** One duel in Greywood (`tools/g4a_shots.js`, `duel-greywood`): the fighter stays the lightest, most contrasted thing in the frame against the canopy and trunks behind it; the canopy grain is soft enough that no strength change was needed (grain depth stays 0.25).
- **Pictures.** `/home/claude/screens/g4a/before` (Build 19's look: `--flattrees`) and `/after`: hearthvale, greywood, conifer, close, close-pine, node, node-pine, duel-greywood. Rerun: `python3 tools/build.py`, then `node tools/g4a_shots.js --modes=before,after --mem`.
- **Weaknesses.** Distant oaks are soft green discs with faint clumps; the lathe canopy silhouette is still a smooth blob (cards were meant to rag it); the canopy grain has one leaf spray per tile, so a close look shows a regular pattern of clumps; pine canopies are very dark under Greywood's ambient; no iPhone check.

## GPU texture formats on the QUALITY screen (lane G4a)
The walls and wood decision depends on compressed textures working on the owner's iPhone. `Run.gpu_textures()` reads `getSupportedExtensions()` once at startup from the engine's own canvas context (`getContext('webgl2')` on a canvas that already has one returns the same context, so no second context is made) and keeps one line: `GPU textures: S3TC ETC2 ASTC BPTC` (the ones present, from `WEBGL_compressed_texture_s3tc`, `_etc`, `_astc`, `EXT_texture_compression_bptc`; `none` when none). It shows under "THE LAND" or "THE TOWER" on the map screen, beside the QUALITY button (15 px label, web only; nothing elsewhere). Headless Chromium (software GL) shows `S3TC ETC2 ASTC BPTC`; an iPhone is expected to show ETC2 and ASTC and not S3TC or BPTC, which is what the owner should report. Also printed as `GPUTEX` in the console with `--perflog`.
