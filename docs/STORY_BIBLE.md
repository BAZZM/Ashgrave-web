## 2. Premise and history

A family is sworn to feed a fire. Two hundred years ago a boy went up the Ashgrave to tend the pyre that ended the Burning; his brother stayed below to keep a hearth lit from it. The fire keeps both alive and burns only on their blood: every heir who dies on the stair is fuel. The brother below is Elder Maren, who has sent thirty descendants up to fetch the boy down or keep him company, and never decided which. The player is the thirty-first. **Central question: is one warm vale worth a family's dead, and who decides: the warm or the burned?**

|When|What|
|---|---|
|Before|A kingdom seated at the Old Ruins; Greywood its forest (Q:29). Its royal house were smiths (S:32)|
|**The Burning**|The king's foundries dug the ember seam under the north. It burned a year; the king died; the sky stayed overcast. Wren's order, the **Sisters of the Mire**, cut cisterns into his tomb-tower to drown it and failed (S:6)|
|The oath|The altar's Keepers found the fire sat wherever royal blood sat. The elder prince, a child, was crowned Regent (S:31), spoke his half at the gate (S:5) and went up. The family swore theirs (Q:53). The younger, Maren, carried a coal down: Hearthvale's hearth|
|The lie|The Regent learned the seam was dead: the fire burned only on the blood (S:33). He wrote "Send no one after me" and went up to starve it. The rooks had gone (S:8)|
|The heirs|Hearing nothing, Maren sent thirty. Each death fed the fire. Maren scratched "A lie." (Q:56) and kept sending|

- **The Ashgrave:** the king's tomb-tower over the ember shaft; his pyre has never gone out.
- **Why heirs climb:** they are told the cause is just (S:21): relieve the keeper.
- **The bloodline** (R:428-440): the oath counts the line as one keeper. An heir who dies on the stair burns; no body returns. The Foundry reforges the arms (S:7), the Cinder Warden lays them at the gate, and the next heir sleeps by the hearth and wakes with the last one's skill (H:646).
- **Defeat outside** (R:436): the debt is owed to the pyre alone, so the blood cannot die elsewhere. Villagers can.
- **Ascension** (R:412): killing the Regent cannot end an oath heirs still keep (S:34). The fire stands the armour up again, hotter.
- **The Ashlands:** the hearth's peace ends at **the ash line**. Every player is a cousin of the blood, and blood cannot kill blood outside the tower, so the loser pays (WH:880). They fight over ember, old holdings and the oath.
- **What it keeps warm** (S:9): two brothers and one vale. Without it Hearthvale gets winter and the brothers die.

## 9. Quest data schema

A superset of today's `QUESTS` entries (Q:9); existing quests stay valid. JSON, so it pastes into GDScript.

```json
{"id": {
  "name": "", "act": 1, "order": 10, "giver": "npc", "auto": false,
  "requires": ["quest:forge", "flag:oath=end", "relic:1", "tier:1", "loop>=1", "!quest:mire"],
  "offer": ["line", {"if": [["bloodline>=2", "line"]], "else": "line"}],
  "accept": "", "decline": "",
  "steps": [{"do": "talk|bring|kill|reach|choose|find|win", "to": "npc", "mats": {}, "foes": [], "need": 1, "place": "", "relic": 1, "tier": 1,
    "text": "journal line", "track": "tracker line", "say": [],
    "choices": [["label", "value"]], "reply": {"value": ["line"]}}],
  "finish": [],
  "reward": {"gold": 0, "xp": {}, "item": "", "tools": 0, "mats": {}, "lore": [], "flags": {}},
  "journal": "closing entry"
}}
```

- **Conditions** read existing save data: `quest` (done), `active`, `flag`, `relic` (R:53), `tier` (`best_floor` > N, R:423), `loop`, `bloodline`, `skill:id>=N`, `item`, `mat`; `!` negates.
- **Variants:** any line or list of lines may be `{"if": [[condition, value]], "else": value}`; first match wins. Per-loop and per-heir text uses this.
- **Steps:** `find` and `win` are state checks, so old saves pass. `choose` stores `flags[id]` (Q:196), then shows `reply` and `finish`.
- **Offers:** an active step for an NPC beats a new offer; offers go by `order` (default 100).
- **Saves only grow:** `Run.data.quests[id]` stays `{step, count, done}` (Q:190). Shipped quests never gain, lose or reorder steps. One new default key: `lore`. The loader drops unknown keys (R:89).
